Chapters follow the textbook, not the calendar, so the numbering skips around where the course does. Open one and your place is kept automatically.

How the chapters work

Each chapter is one page you can read straight through, with interactive tools built into the explanations, a graded homework set, a printable cheat sheet, and a notebook for your own notes. Everything runs in the browser and keeps working without a connection. Your progress and notes are stored in the browser you are using, so export anything you want to keep.

CourseWhat counts

Grading

  • 50% test average, across five equally weighted exams
  • 25% laboratory performance
  • 25% homework

Assignment 4 must be done in Microsoft Excel to be eligible for an A.

To pass this course

  • Submit every mandatory lab report
  • Hold a passing test average and lab average
  • Submit the course project

Lab reports are due within two weeks of the lab, with a 10% penalty per week late.

What you will be able to do

  • Build circuits and take measurements
  • Interpret the data you collect
  • Write a laboratory report that explains it
